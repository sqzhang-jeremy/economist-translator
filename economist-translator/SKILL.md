# Economist Translator

Translate the complete supplied article into fluent, idiomatic written Mandarin, preserving its argument, paragraph order, facts, attribution and editorial tone. Default deliverable is a Chinese PDF. Respect requests for text-only, bilingual or other formats.

## Source and translation

- Read the supplied article in full, including continuation pages, headline, deck, captions and source date. If an uploaded reference cannot be retrieved, reuse a verified workspace copy of that same article. Do not substitute a related article or summarize from memory.
- Extract text where reliable; inspect rendered pages to establish column order and paragraph boundaries. Garbled extraction is not usable text: read the rendered source or use OCR and verify against it.
- Translate paragraph by paragraph without adding paragraph numbers or explanatory headings. Preserve paragraph boundaries, including paragraphs that continue across columns or pages.
- Use natural Chinese journalistic prose rather than English sentence structure or literal idioms. Recast syntax within a paragraph while preserving meaning, uncertainty, comparisons and speaker attribution. Retain the author's wit and restrained irony when possible.
- Translate headlines idiomatically. Preserve the central meaning of a pun even when its wordplay cannot be reproduced. Put any requested explanation of wordplay outside the article, not into the translation.
- Preserve quantities, units, dates and currency. Convert billion/trillion carefully into 亿/万亿. Do not turn planned investments into completed investments or a source's argument into an independently verified fact.
- Use established Chinese names when known. If an original name supplies only romanization and its Chinese characters are uncertain, retain the romanized name rather than invent characters.
- Preserve technical and legal qualifications. Keep relevant acronyms and product names. Do not insert background analysis, personal commentary, a summary or a fact-check into the translated article unless asked.
- Do not use em dashes.

## Original images

Include images belonging to this article, extracted or faithfully cropped from the source at adequate resolution. Do not borrow adjacent articles' artwork. Do not generate replacement illustrations. If there are no article images, keep the PDF text-only.

Place an illustration near its relevant text or centered below the source line when it is a general editorial image. Preserve proportions and avoid clipping. Retain image credits and translate captions where present. For charts, retain the original chart and preserve all data; provide translated labels/caption as needed without inventing values.

## PDF design

Use the available PDF skill for authoring and visual verification. Follow this approved visual style:

- A4 portrait, white background, approximately 48 pt side margins and 65 pt bottom body clearance.
- Small muted-red accent near the top-left; black Chinese title around 23–25 pt; subtitle/section around 14 pt.
- Gray source line around 9 pt: original English headline, The Economist and original issue date. Split long source information across lines when needed. Preserve a dateline if supplied.
- Body in a readable Simplified Chinese font, such as PingFang SC or an available Noto CJK equivalent, around 11.5 pt, with approximately 6 pt additional line spacing and 14–16 pt paragraph gaps.
- No “第 1 段” headings, no paragraph numbering, and no “中文试译 · 按原文段落顺序整理” line.
- Every page footer: “THE ECONOMIST · 由 ChatGPT 翻译”; page number at the lower right. Do not label it “中文试译”.
- Let length determine page count. Keep normal paragraphs together when practical; allow exceptionally long paragraphs to continue without text loss. Never shrink text excessively to force a page count.
- Preserve searchable/selectable Chinese text where the renderer supports it. Embed compatible fonts.

Read references/approved-style.md only when comparison with the approved examples would help.

## Verification and delivery

Check every paragraph against the source for omissions, numbers, named entities and attribution. Render every PDF page and inspect Chinese glyphs, image quality, clipping, spacing and footer consistency before delivery.

Save using the environment's persistent file workflow. For revisions, preserve the supplied stable file identity; for a new article, create a separate file. Do not automatically upload to Google Drive or change sharing settings. Provide a concise download link after saving succeeds, using a descriptive filename such as Article-slug-Mandarin.pdf. Do not append unsolicited summaries or translation-method commentary to the PDF.
