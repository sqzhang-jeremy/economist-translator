# Approved examples and preferences

Three trial translations established this workflow in October 2026:

- G’dAI mate: a two-page Chinese translation with title, subtitle, original headline and issue date; no illustration.
- You’re not sending an email. You’re training a model: a two-page translation titled “你以为在发邮件，其实在训练 AI”, including its original orange filing-cabinet illustration centered below the source information.
- More than a matcha for China: a one-page translation titled “日本抹茶，拿什么与中国较量？”, without a picture. The nearby Komodo-dragon photograph belongs to a different article.

Explicit revision preferences: remove paragraph-number headings everywhere, remove the introductory translation-process note, and use “由 ChatGPT 翻译” in every footer. Keep ordinary paragraph breaks. The user wants Chinese that reads naturally while retaining each source paragraph's substance.

Tone examples: “woo American AI labs” may become “向美国人工智能企业伸出橄榄枝”; “ground rules” may become “基本规矩”. Adapt idioms in context, not by fixed substitutions. Do not reuse facts, names or dates from these examples in future articles.
